Benzoloader FAQ

Answers about the app and the developer.

1What is Benzoloader?

An Android download manager written in Kotlin (package khaled.benzo). It downloads files from web links with several connections at once, can pause and resume, handles torrents (.torrent files and magnet links), and has a built-in browser that detects media on pages. It comes in English and Arabic.

2Who makes it?

Khaled Hassan, an independent developer and music producer from Egypt. He builds Android apps and AI developer tools and publishes them on GitHub as Khaledhassan12 (github.com/Khaledhassan12).

He also makes music under the name Kha Led Benzema, a music producer and composer channel on YouTube (youtube.com/@spyhuman9273).

3Is it free? Are there ads?

It is free. There are no ads in the app and none are planned.

Blocking ads on websites you open in the browser is a separate feature and has nothing to do with the app showing ads.

4Where do I get it?

As an APK on GitHub: github.com/Khaledhassan12/Benzoloader. A Google Play version is planned for later.

5What permissions does it ask for, and why?
  • Notifications: download progress and results.
  • Storage: saving your files. The GitHub version can optionally ask for "All files access" so it can save to any folder you choose. You can decline and use the folders Android allows.
  • Display over other apps: only for the Top window, and only if you turn it on.
  • Network and Wi-Fi state: to download, and for the Wi-Fi only rule.
  • Foreground service, wake lock, alarms, start at boot: to keep downloads running, run the scheduler and resume the queue.
  • Vibration: for the vibration setting.
  • Install packages: to open a finished APK you downloaded.
6Where are my downloads saved?

In the folder you choose in Settings. Benzoloader never changes the storage location without you choosing it.

7What is the Top window?

A small bar shown over other apps while a download runs. Text colour, background, opacity, width and what it shows are set in Settings > Top window. It appears only after you turn it on.

8What are the Queue and Finished lists, and Single list?

By default the main screen has two lists, Queue and Finished. Turn on Single list (Settings > Interface, or the left drawer) to see everything in one list.

The Bottom action bar switch (same two places) shows or hides the bar at the bottom of the screen.

Long press a download to open its menu.

9Does it keep my browsing history?

Only if you switch on "remember visited pages" or "keep open tabs" in Settings > Browser. Both are off by default.

10Is my data sent anywhere?

Benzoloader has no accounts and no analytics or advertising code. Settings and the download list are stored on your phone. Exporting settings saves app settings only, never passwords.

Your downloads and the in-app browser use the internet, of course, to reach the sites and torrent peers you choose.

11How fast is it? Is resume always safe?

No speed claims are made. Speed depends on the server and your network. Resuming works when the server supports it. The app has not yet been measured on many devices.

12Something is wrong, or I have a request

Use the ? button at the top of the main screen: Chat opens WhatsApp with the developer, Email opens your mail app with a ready message to khaled.hassansadik@gmail.com.

Please write what you did, what you expected and what happened, and attach a screenshot if you can. Your phone model, Android version and app version are filled into the draft for you to see and edit. Nothing is sent until you press send.
